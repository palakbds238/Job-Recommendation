About CareerAI

A job-matching platform that explains why a job fits you, shows which skills you are missing, and lets issuers prove your credentials without exposing your data.

How matching works

  • Skills first. Every job is scored on how many of its required skills you have. Skills are normalised, so "JS" matches "JavaScript".
  • Text and meaning. Your profile and resume are compared with each job description (TF-IDF similarity, and optional sentence embeddings).
  • Category fit. A Random Forest model predicts which job category suits your profile.
  • People like you. Collaborative filtering uses what similar users saved or applied to — it stays quiet until there is enough data.
  • Transparent. Each recommendation shows the score breakdown and the reason it was suggested.

Verified credentials

An authorised issuer (for example a university) can issue a credential to you. Only a salted fingerprint (hash) is recorded on a blockchain, never your name or documents. Anyone can then check that a credential is genuine and not revoked from its verification link, and skills backed by a valid credential rank higher.

Job data

Jobs come from a curated sample dataset, from jobs added by administrators and, when enabled, from live listings provided by Adzuna and Remotive. Live listings link back to the original advertisement.

CareerAI is a college project built to demonstrate machine learning and blockchain techniques. Match scores are guidance, not a guarantee of any hiring outcome.
